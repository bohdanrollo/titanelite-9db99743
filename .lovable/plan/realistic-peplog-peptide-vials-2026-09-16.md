# Realistic PepLog peptide vials

## Goal
Replace the current peptide imagery in the client dashboard’s Peptide Research tab with a realistic vial based on the supplied reference, while preserving all existing search, filtering, research details, and access behavior.

## Implementation
- Create a clean, reusable PepLog vial photograph from the supplied reference, keeping the glass, cap, lighting, black label, and cyan/white visual language.
- Update each peptide tile to place its real compound name, strength, category, research-use notice, and generated lot number cleanly on the vial label.
- Add length-aware typography so short and long peptide names remain centered, legible, and contained within the label.
- Keep the surrounding peptide library layout and all dashboard functionality unchanged.

## Verification
- Review representative short, medium, and long peptide names at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm labels do not overlap or clip, images remain realistic, and the Peptide Research interactions still work.
